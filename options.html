About

Information about this extension

How It Works

LitRPG Release Watch monitors Audible and Amazon for updates to your favorite book series. It checks for:

  • New books in the series
  • Release date changes
  • Availability changes (pre-order → available)
  • Narrator or runtime changes (Audible only)

Privacy

All data is stored locally on your device. No information is sent to external servers. No analytics or tracking.

Tips

  • Use specific search URLs from Audible/Amazon for best results
  • Enable fallback scraping if you encounter bot detection
  • Adjust date shift threshold to avoid noise from minor changes
  • Export your data regularly as a backup
